Our Statutory Engineering & Delivery Framework

A disciplined 4-stage engineering delivery process engineered to transition your project from initial architectural layouts to municipal sanction clearances and site-ready Good for Construction (GFC) drawings on time and error-free.

01
Phase 1

Architectural Intake & DBR Formulation

We kick off every engagement by formulating the Design Basis Report (DBR). We review architectural models, municipal master plans, setback guidelines, FAR/FSI constraints, state DISCOM incoming power availability, and environmental clearances (EC). We execute mutual NDAs and set up collaborative cloud environments.

Design Basis Report (DBR) Signed Mutual NDA Local Municipal Bye-Law Matrix DISCOM & Fire NOC Milestones
02
Phase 2

System Sizing, Load Calculations & Schematics

Our senior discipline leads execute Carrier HAP cooling calculations under ISHRAE guidelines, electrical fault current and transformer sizing per IS 732, hydro-pneumatic water booster calculations per NBC Part 9, and hydraulic fire pump modeling per NBC Part 4. We prepare single-line diagrams (SLDs) and plant room footprints.

Carrier HAP Cooling Loads 11kV/33kV Substation Sizing STP & Rainwater Harvesting Calcs BIM Execution Plan (BEP)
03
Phase 3

3D Revit Modeling & Statutory Sanction Sets

Production takes place in native Autodesk Revit. Mechanical ductwork, electrical cable trays, plumbing waste/supply risers, and fire hydrants/sprinklers are modeled to LOD 300-350. We run weekly automated Navisworks clash resolution tests to guarantee zero beam clashes and release formal drawings for municipal, Fire NOC, and CEIG approvals.

Revit LOD 300 - 350 Modeling Navisworks Clash Matrices Municipal Sanction Drawings Fire NOC Escape & Hydrant Sets
04
Phase 4

GFC Drawing Release & CPWD DSR Bill of Quantities

Prior to site release, all deliverables undergo a 3-tier quality assurance check. Deliverables include stamped Good for Construction (GFC) installation drawings, deck penetration sleeve sets, prefabrication duct/pipe spools, and itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ) aligned with CPWD Delhi Schedule of Rates (DSR).

Stamped GFC Drawing Sets Deck Sleeve & Penetration Plans CPWD DSR Aligned BOQ / QTO On-Site Execution Support
