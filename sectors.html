Engineering Excellence for India's Growth Sectors

From massive commercial IT tech parks and luxury high-rise towers to super-specialty hospitals and industrial logistics parks, Stratum provides tailored MEP design and 3D BIM coordination compliant with NBC 2016, IS Codes, and local municipal bye-laws.

IT Parks, SEZs & Tech Campuses

High-density occupant loads across Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, and Delhi NCR. We engineer energy-optimized water-cooled central chiller plants, high-efficiency primary-secondary pumping, 11kV/33kV indoor substations, N+1 DG synchronizing sets, and ECBC/IGBC Platinum compliant LED photometrics.

ECBC 2017 & IGBC Platinum Water-Cooled Chiller Plants 11kV/33kV Substation Yards

High-Rise Residential Towers & Townships

Overcoming the challenges of static head pressure, vertical riser expansion, and sound attenuation in 30+ to 60+ story towers in Mumbai, Gurgaon, and Noida. Multi-zone hydro-pneumatic water boosting, pressure reducing stations (PRV), acoustic drain piping, and pressurized fire escape stairs.

NBC 2016 Part 4 Fire NOC Multi-Zone PHE Risers Rooftop VRV/VRF Networks

Hospitals & Super-Specialty Healthcare

Critical life-support environments: sterile operating room laminar airflow under NABH guidelines, negative-pressure airborne isolation suites, medical gas pipeline systems (MGPS: Oxygen, Nitrous, Vacuum per HTM 02-01 / IS), isolated power supply (IPS) for ICUs, and 100% emergency backup.

NABH & ISHRAE Healthcare Medical Gas Manifold (MGPS) OT Laminar Airflow & HEPA

Retail Shopping Malls & Multiplexes

Dynamic occupancy swings and diverse tenant fit-out requirements. Centralized chilled water BTU metering for individual tenant billing, basement CO jet fan ventilation, atrium smoke evacuation modeling, and commercial food court grease interceptors with make-up air interlocks.

Tenant BTU Energy Metering Food Court Exhaust & Grease Traps Atrium Smoke Management

Industrial Facilities, Warehouses & Logistics Hubs

Large-span manufacturing plants and grade-A logistics parks in Chakan, Bhiwandi, and Sri City. High-velocity roof ventilation, high-expansion foam fire suppression for chemical storage, ESFR (Early Suppression Fast Response) ceiling sprinklers, and effluent treatment plants (ETP).

ESFR High-Bay Sprinklers Zero Liquid Discharge (ETP) Heavy Power Industrial HT Yards

Data Centers & Mission-Critical Facilities

Tier III and Tier IV data center design in Navi Mumbai, Chennai, and Noida. Redundant 2N transformer-DG architectures, dual-corded static transfer switches (STS), hot/cold aisle containment, CRAH/CRAC computer room cooling, and clean agent gas suppression (FM-200 / Novec 1230).

Tier III / IV 2N Redundancy Gas Fire Flooding (Novec 1230) Hot Aisle CFD Thermal Modeling
Pan-India Deployment
