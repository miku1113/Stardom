MEP Engineering Services

We design heating, ventilation and air conditioning (HVAC), power, plumbing and fire protection systems. Choose the disciplines your project needs.

View services

Four Design Disciplines

HVAC

We calculate heating and cooling loads, size equipment and plan how air moves through the building.

Cooling load calculations and equipment schedules
Chiller and variable refrigerant flow (VRF) systems
Ductwork, chilled water piping and basement ventilation

Electrical

We design power distribution from the utility connection through to equipment, outlets and lighting.

Substation and transformer layouts
Power and lighting layouts, single-line diagrams and load schedules
Backup generators, earthing and lightning protection

Plumbing

We plan how water enters, moves through and leaves the building.

Water supply, storage, pumping and hot water
Wastewater and stormwater drainage
Sewage treatment, water reuse and rainwater harvesting

Fire Protection

We design fire protection systems and prepare drawings for fire authority review.

Sprinklers, hydrants and fire alarm layouts
Fire pump and tank sizing
Smoke control and stairwell pressurisation

What You Receive

We agree the drawing set and level of detail at the start of the project.

Design Calculations

Load estimates, equipment sizing and schematics for the systems in scope.

Coordinated Layouts

Plans and sections that show how ducts, cables and pipes fit around the structure and each other.

Submission Drawings

Drawing sets for building, fire and electrical authority review, as required by the project.

Construction Drawings

Installation details, equipment schedules and a bill of quantities for tendering or construction.
