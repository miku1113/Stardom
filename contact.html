1

General MEP Consultancy

MEP stands for Mechanical, Electrical and Plumbing. It covers the essential building engineering systems, including HVAC, electrical distribution, lighting, fire protection, plumbing, drainage and other building services.

An MEP consultant provides professional engineering design, planning, technical documentation, system coordination and consultancy services to ensure building services are safe, efficient, reliable and compliant with applicable standards.

We provide MEP consultancy services for residential, commercial, industrial, hospitality, healthcare, institutional and infrastructure-related projects.

Ideally, an MEP consultant should be appointed during the initial planning and concept design stage. Early involvement helps optimize system design, reduce coordination conflicts and avoid unnecessary project costs.

Drawings & Coordination

We prepare:

  • Conceptual MEP Layouts
  • Detailed MEP Design Drawings
  • 2D Coordinated MEP Layouts
  • HVAC Ducting and Piping Layouts
  • Electrical and Lighting Layouts
  • Plumbing and Drainage Layouts
  • Fire Fighting and Fire Alarm Layouts
  • Equipment Room Layouts
  • Schematic Diagrams and Details

MEP coordination is the process of integrating mechanical, electrical and plumbing systems with architectural and structural elements to minimize clashes, improve installation efficiency and ensure smooth project execution.

We can support BIM-based coordination and engineering workflows according to project scope and requirements.

MEP design focuses on engineering calculations, system selection and technical design. MEP coordination focuses on integrating the designed systems with architectural and structural layouts to resolve spatial and installation conflicts.
