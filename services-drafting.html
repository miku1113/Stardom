Streamlined Delivery

Our Drafting Workflow

A systematic production cycle ensuring drawing consistency across thousands of sheets.

01

Local Bye-Law Intake

We review your site plan, local master plan zoning, setback guidelines, FAR tables, and municipal templates to establish clean drafting parameters.

02

Drafting & Model Setup

Our experienced drafters set up models in AutoCAD or Revit, establishing shared coordinates, project grids, levels, and linking external disciplines.

03

Annotation & Multidiscipline Check

We cross-check door swings against structural posts, verify ceiling soffit heights for duct drops, and complete comprehensive dimension strings.

04

Quality Audit & Delivery

A senior reviewer validates line weights, xref paths, plot styles (CTB), and sheet indices before final PDF and DWG transmission.

Common Questions

Drafting Services FAQs

Common questions regarding our architectural, sanction, and structural drafting workflows.

Can you prepare sanction drawings according to our local municipal authority?

+

Yes. We have detailed knowledge of municipal bye-laws across major Indian metropolitan regions including DDA (Delhi), BMC (Mumbai), BBMP (Bengaluru), NOIDA/Greater NOIDA, and GHMC (Hyderabad). All setbacks, FAR/FSI calculations, fire driveways, and parking layouts are formatted to the exact authority templates.

Do your structural drawings comply with Indian Seismic codes?

+

Yes. Our structural drafting adheres strictly to IS 456 (concrete), IS 800 (steel), and IS 13920 (ductile detailing of reinforced concrete structures subjected to seismic forces), essential for building projects in seismic zones III, IV, and V across India.

How do you coordinate architectural plans with MEP services?

+

As a multi-disciplinary MEP and VDC consultancy, our architectural and structural drafting teams cross-reference all HVAC duct drops, plumbing chases, electrical riser shafts, and structural slab sleeves before GFC release, ensuring zero clashes on site.

What is your billing structure for drafting support?

+

We offer flexible models: fixed-fee lump sum per project milestone, or dedicated monthly drafter squads where engineers work exclusively on your firm's pipeline for a predictable flat monthly rate.
