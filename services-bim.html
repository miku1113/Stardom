Streamlined Delivery

Our BIM Production Workflow

A step-by-step BIM production workflow engineered to deliver coordinated, high-quality Revit models efficiently.

01

BIM Execution Plan (BEP) & Setup

Review project requirements, templates, LOD specifications, and discipline scopes. Set up shared coordinates, linked models, naming conventions, worksets, and view templates to establish a standardized BIM workspace.

02

Discipline Modeling

Model Architectural, Structural, and MEP components to the specified LOD using agreed family libraries and parameter standards, maintaining alignment with project design intent and structural grids.

03

QC & Clash Resolution

Run automated clash detection tests in Navisworks Manage, identify interferences between MEP trades and structure, and resolve routing conflicts collaboratively through weekly coordination cycles.

04

Final Delivery & Cloud Sync

Conduct multi-tier internal model audits, generate permit/construction documentation sheets, and deliver clean, coordinated Revit models (.RVT), Navisworks files (.NWD), and PDF sheet sets synced to your ACC cloud.
