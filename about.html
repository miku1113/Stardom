Who We Are

A Premier Multi-Disciplinary Engineering & VDC Consultancy

Headquartered in Noida (Delhi NCR) with an advanced engineering and VDC center in Koramangala, Bengaluru, Stratum bridges the critical gap between conceptual architecture, statutory clearance, and zero-defect site execution.

Our teams comprise licensed Chartered Engineers, IGBC/GRIHA accredited professionals, and certified Autodesk Revit specialists. We have delivered over 250 landmark projects totaling 15M+ sq. ft. across 18 Indian states. By combining deep mastery of the National Building Code of India (NBC 2016), Indian Standards (IS), and CPWD specifications with international BIM protocols (ISO 19650), we guarantee seamless constructability and 99.8% first-round authority approvals.
