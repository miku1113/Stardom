Streamlined Delivery

Our MEP Engineering Workflow

A predictable 4-stage engineering delivery process aligned with Indian municipal sanctions, CEIG approvals, and contractor site execution.

01

Architectural Intake & DBR Formulation

Analyze architectural BIM/CAD models, local municipal bye-laws (FAR, setbacks), DISCOM incoming HT power availability, and formulate the Design Basis Report (DBR) aligned with NBC 2016 and ECBC.

02

System Sizing & Schematic Development

Run Carrier HAP cooling loads, electrical fault calculations, hydro-pneumatic riser sizing, and fire pump hydraulic modeling. Prepare SLDs, riser diagrams, and plant room spatial allocations.

03

Multi-Trade 3D Coordination & Sanction Sets

Model all MEP services in Autodesk Revit. Run multi-trade Navisworks clash tests to guarantee zero on-site beam/conduit clashes. Finalize drawings for Fire NOC, CEIG, and municipal sanctions.

04

GFC Drawing Release & Site BOQ Support

Deliver stamped Good for Construction (GFC) drawings, fabrication spool cut sheets, sleeve penetration layouts, and itemized CPWD DSR aligned Bill of Quantities (BOQ).

Common Questions

MEP Design FAQs

Frequently asked questions regarding Indian statutory clearances, standards, and delivery models.

How do you ensure drawings pass municipal Fire Department NOC scrutiny?

+

Our life safety engineering adheres strictly to NBC 2016 Part 4 and state-specific Fire Prevention and Life Safety Measures Acts (e.g. Delhi, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana). We design static water storage, booster pump capacities, travel distances, compartmentation, and pressurized fire escape stairs to guarantee hassle-free NOC clearance.

Can you design electrical substations for State DISCOM and CEIG approvals?

+

Yes. We prepare complete 11kV/33kV substation layouts, transformer yard sections, clearance diagrams, single-line diagrams, relay coordination, and earthing pit calculations compliant with Central Electricity Authority (CEA) regulations, IS 732, IS 3043, and local State Chief Electrical Inspectorate (CEIG) requirements.

Do your designs meet IGBC / GRIHA green building rating benchmarks?

+

Absolutely. We incorporate energy-efficient chillers, VFDs on pumps and fans, heat recovery wheels, LED photometrics under ECBC lighting power densities, STP zero-liquid-discharge water recycling, and CGWA rainwater harvesting systems required for Gold and Platinum green certifications.

What is your typical turnaround time for an Indian MEP project?

+

Design Basis Reports (DBR) and schematic load calculations are typically ready in 3 to 5 business days. Complete statutory sanction sets or tender drawing packages for mid-to-large commercial complexes (50,000 to 500,000 sq ft) are delivered in 2 to 3 weeks with milestone-based staging.
