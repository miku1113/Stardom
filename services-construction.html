Construction Support Workflow

From coordinated 3D Revit models to fabrication shop floor and job site sign-off.

01

Model Audit & Spool Setup

Review coordinated Navisworks model and structural background drawings. Define contractor spool boundaries, shipping truck length limitations, and shop weld standards.

02

Spool Generation & Detailing

Generate detailed isometric spool sheets with cut lengths, weld mappings, hanger elevations, and tagged bills of materials using fabrication add-ins.

03

Sleeve Layouts & Grid Referencing

Produce deck penetration layouts dimensioned from column centerlines, verifying clearances against rebar, post-tension cables, and structural beams.

04

Quality Control & Field Release

Perform multi-tier QA/QC checks against shop fabrication tolerances before releasing signed PDF and DXF/DWG sets directly to shop floor foremen.

Common Questions

Construction Support FAQs

Answers to key questions regarding contractor shop drawings and spool services.

What information do you need to generate pipe spool drawings?

+

We need your approved 3D coordinated Revit/CAD model, your preferred spool length constraints (e.g. maximum length based on delivery truck beds or elevator access), joint types (welded, grooved, threaded, or flanged), and your firm's sheet border template.

How do you coordinate sleeve drawings with post-tensioned (PT) cables?

+

We overlay the structural engineer's PT cable tendon profiles and rebar shop drawings onto our MEP layout. We maintain the required clearance from primary tendons and place sleeve centerlines with exact offsets from structural grid lines.

Can you create custom Revit families for Indian equipment manufacturers?

+

Yes. We build manufacturer-grade Revit families (.RFA) for Indian HVAC, pump, and electrical brands (Blue Star, Voltas, Kirloskar, ABB, Havells). Families include accurate MEP system connectors, electrical ratings, clearance envelopes, and type catalogs (.TXT).

Are your quantity takeoff (QTO) estimates aligned with CPWD norms?

+

Because our QTOs are extracted directly from coordinated 3D BIM models, linear meterage and fitting counts are within 98-99% accuracy. We itemize components by trade, material specification, diameter, and floor level, formatted to CPWD Delhi Schedule of Rates (DSR) or custom contractor formats.
