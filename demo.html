How We Work

A systematic, stage-gated engineering workflow from initial architectural drawings to clash-free site construction sets. Click any stage to inspect deliverables.

Set the Scope

Review architectural drawings, building occupancy, and local statutory requirements (NBC 2016, IS, ECBC).

Project Brief Design Basis Drawing Schedule

Size the Systems

Calculate thermal cooling loads, electrical demand, water supply flow, and fire pump capacity to size primary equipment.

Heat Load Sizing Equipment Schedules Single Line Diagrams

Coordinate Layouts

Prepare integrated 2D MEP coordination layouts for HVAC ducts, plumbing pipes, electrical cable trays, and fire-fighting services, ensuring proper routing, clearances, and coordination with architectural and structural drawings.

Integrated 2D Layouts MEP Coordination Drawings Coordinated Overlays Shaft Clearances

Issue the Drawings

Release authority submission drawings, Good For Construction (GFC) sets, sleeve cutouts, and tender bill of quantities.
