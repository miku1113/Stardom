— STRUCTURED DELIVERY FRAMEWORK

Our MEP Engineering Process.

A disciplined, transparent 4-stage engineering framework that advances your project from concept through statutory sanction approvals and Good-For-Construction (GFC) release.

01

Client Brief & Statutory Review

Collect architectural layouts, verify building classification under NBC 2016 Part 3, and establish local municipal development authority bye-laws (DDA, BMC, BBMP, NOIDA).

Architectural model & master plan audit
NBC classification & local bye-law matrix
Design basis report (DBR) sign-off
02

Calculations & BEP Plan

Perform Carrier HAP / TRACE cooling loads for Indian ambient temperatures, calculate maximum electrical demand per IS 732, and establish the Revit BIM Execution Plan (BEP).

HAP cooling loads & ECBC compliance
DISCOM HT/LT transformer & DG sizing
STP, ETP, and RWH tank volumetric calcs
03

Detailed Design & Coordination

Model all MEP services in Autodesk Revit LOD 350-400, run automated Navisworks clash tests with structural elements, and design riser shafts and ceiling distributions.

3D parametric MEP model in Revit
Navisworks multi-trade clash resolution
Sleeve openings & builder cutout drawings
04

Statutory Approvals & GFC Release

Conduct senior engineering peer review, assemble municipal sanction packages (Fire NOC, DISCOM clearance), and release signed Good-For-Construction (GFC) drawings and BOQs.

Fire NOC & authority sanction sheet sets
Good-For-Construction (GFC) drawing release
Detailed CPWD-aligned itemized BOQ
