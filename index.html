How We Work

From the first floor plan to construction drawings.

01

Set the scope

Review the brief, building plans and local requirements.

02

Size the systems

Calculate loads and choose equipment to suit the building.

03

Coordinate the layouts

Check where ducts, pipes and cables cross other services or structure.

04

Issue the drawings

Prepare submission and construction drawings, plus a bill of quantities where required.
