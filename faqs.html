MEP Design FAQs

Answers about MEP scope, drawings for approvals and what to share when you request a proposal.

Scope and Deliverables

MEP stands for mechanical, electrical and plumbing. Choose mechanical, electrical, or plumbing design based on your project. Fire protection design is listed under Other Services.

Depending on the scope, we provide system layouts, equipment schedules, drawings for approvals and drawings for construction. We can also prepare a bill of quantities for tendering.

Yes. We compare service layouts with the building plans and structure so the team can resolve conflicts before construction. We can work in 2D or with a shared 3D model.

Approvals and Standards

Yes. We can prepare documents for municipal, fire and electrical authority review. The authority decides whether to grant approval.

We review the National Building Code of India, applicable Indian Standards and local authority requirements for the project location and building use.

Share the rating system and targets at the start. We can consider options such as efficient cooling, rainwater harvesting and water reuse in the design.

Starting a Project

Send the building type, location, floor area, current design stage and target dates. If you have floor plans or a tender brief, tell us when you send the enquiry.

Timing depends on building size, the drawings you need and how much coordination the design requires. We agree dates for each stage once we review the scope.
