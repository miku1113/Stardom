Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding MEP engineering consultancy, NBC 2016 & IS code compliance, statutory municipal approvals (Fire NOC, DISCOM, CEIG), Revit BIM coordination, and project delivery across India.

1. Statutory Approvals, Code Compliance & Municipal Sanctions

All engineering calculations, schematics, and layouts comply strictly with the National Building Code of India (NBC 2016), applicable Bureau of Indian Standards (IS) codes (including IS 732 for electrical wiring, IS 3043 for earthing systems, IS 2189 for automatic fire detection, IS 1239 & IS 1536 for water and drainage piping, IS 3844 for internal hydrants, and IS 15105 for automatic sprinkler installations), Energy Conservation Building Code (ECBC 2017), Central Public Works Department (CPWD) specifications, and local municipal bye-laws (DDA, BMC, BBMP, NOIDA, GHMC, DTCP).

Yes. We engineer complete statutory drawing submissions and technical calculation dossiers required for State Fire and Emergency Services Provisional & Final NOCs (fire pump sizing, evacuation egress travel distance calculations per NBC Part 4), Chief Electrical Inspectorate to Government (CEIG / CEA) clearances for transformer installations and HT switchgear, and municipal DISCOMs (such as BSES, Tata Power, BESCOM, MSEDCL, UPPCL) for 11kV/33kV power load sanctions and dual-DG synchronization.

Our mechanical and plumbing engineering teams incorporate high-efficiency water-cooled chillers with low IPLV, variable primary pumping, dedicated outdoor air systems (DOAS) with sensible and latent heat recovery wheels, rainwater harvesting recharge pits designed to Central Ground Water Authority (CGWA) guidelines, zero liquid discharge (ZLD) on-site Sewage Treatment Plants (MBBR/SBR technology), low-flow aerated sanitary fixtures, and rooftop solar PV net-metering integration to ensure maximum energy and water credits.

2. Engineering Disciplines, Sizing & Technical Rigor

Our complete engineering package encompasses the initial Design Basis Report (DBR), schematic Single Line Diagrams (SLDs) for electrical distribution, peak heat load calculations (Carrier HAP/Trane TRACE), hydraulic pump head calculations, riser diagrams, tender-ready Bills of Quantities (BOQ) aligned with CPWD Delhi Schedule of Rates (DSR), detailed technical specifications, vendor pre-qualification criteria, and coordinated Good For Construction (GFC) drawings.

We design complete indoor and outdoor substations, including dry-type and oil-immersed step-down transformers, HT vacuum circuit breakers (VCB), Main Low Tension (MLT) distribution panels with motorized Air Circuit Breakers (ACBs), automatic power factor correction (APFC) capacitor banks, emergency diesel generator sets sized to N+1 redundancy with acoustic enclosures adhering to CPCB-IV+ emission norms, centralized UPS battery backup rooms, and maintenance-free chemical earthing grids per IS 3043.

We engineer domestic and flushing dual-distribution networks, variable-speed hydro-pneumatic booster pumping systems, gravity storm water drainage, centralized hot water generation (heat pumps/solar thermal), and on-site Sewage Treatment Plants (STP) using advanced Moving Bed Biofilm Reactor (MBBR) or Sequential Batch Reactor (SBR) processes. Treated effluent is polished via tertiary ultrafiltration and UV disinfection for dual-flush recycling and landscape irrigation.

3. BIM (LOD 100-500), Clash Coordination & Site Support

We construct intelligent 3D Revit models for all trades up to LOD 400/500, federate them with the structural RCC model and architectural layouts in Autodesk Navisworks Manage, and conduct rigorous hard and soft clash matrix audits. We issue weekly BIM Collaboration Format (BCF) clash reports, coordinate service crossings, ensure required maintenance access clearances, and resolve clashes virtually before concrete is poured, eliminating expensive site modifications.

Yes. Once virtual clash coordination is approved, our team extracts high-precision Good For Construction (GFC) sheets, builders work drawings identifying all RCC wall and slab sleeve penetrations, MEP coordinate layouts with elevation tags, and HVAC sheet metal / piping spool fabrication cut sheets directly from the federated BIM model.

Yes. While our headquarters in Noida and regional center in Bengaluru serve major developers and contractors across India, our dedicated Virtual Design and Construction (VDC) team has over 12 years of experience supporting global EPC firms across the US, UK, and Middle East conforming to US codes (IBC, IMC, IPC, NEC, NFPA), British/European Standards (BS EN), and Gulf municipal regulations.

We execute mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) prior to receiving any project data or architectural drawings. All digital assets are managed inside encrypted, role-based cloud environments (Autodesk Construction Cloud / ISO 27001-compliant servers) with watermarking, zero third-party disclosure, and biometric physical security across our engineering production floors.
