MEP Design Notes

Four things to check while there is still room to change the plans.

Plan Space for Ducts Early

Check ceiling depth and shaft space before the floor plans are fixed. Include room for insulation, access panels and maintenance.

HVAC design →

List Loads That Need Backup Power

Separate the loads that must stay on during an outage from those that can wait. That list helps size the generator and plan the distribution.

Electrical design →

Plan Plumbing Around the Floor Layout

Group wet areas where possible and leave clear paths for drainage. On tall buildings, agree where pressure zones and pump rooms will go.

Plumbing design →

Leave Room for Fire Protection

Review fire access, escape routes, water storage and pump room space with your design team before plans are fixed.
